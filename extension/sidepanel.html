    Privacy and data

    Your key stays on this device. It’s saved in Chrome local storage and never sent to Distill’s servers.

    Paper text goes straight to your AI provider. When you ask for a summary, explanation, or analysis, the relevant text is sent over HTTPS from your browser directly to your chosen provider (Groq, Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic) using your key — Distill has no server in the middle.

    Local model = fully private. With Ollama / LM Studio, paper text stays on your computer.

    Gemini free-tier note. Google may use content sent on the free tier to improve its products. For sensitive documents, use a paid key or Anthropic instead.

    Distill saves your progress, summaries, highlights and check-ins per page on this device so you can resume. Nothing leaves your browser. Recent pages are kept; old ones are pruned automatically.
